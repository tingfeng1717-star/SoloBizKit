Profit Margin Calculator

Calculate your gross profit, gross profit margin and markup from your business revenue and cost of goods sold.

What Is Gross Profit Margin?

Gross profit margin measures how much money your business keeps from revenue after paying the direct costs of producing or purchasing the goods or services sold.

A higher gross profit margin generally means more revenue remains after covering direct costs. This amount can then contribute toward operating expenses and profit.

How to Calculate Gross Profit Margin

Gross Profit = Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold

Gross Profit Margin = (Gross Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100

Example Calculation

Suppose your business has these figures:

  • Revenue: $10,000
  • Cost of goods sold: $6,000
  • Gross profit: $4,000

Divide the $4,000 gross profit by $10,000 revenue and multiply by 100.

Your gross profit margin is 40%.

Gross Profit Margin vs. Markup

Gross profit margin and markup are related but different. Gross profit margin compares profit with revenue, while markup compares profit with the cost of goods sold.

For example, a $4,000 gross profit on $6,000 in costs represents a markup of approximately 66.67%, while the gross profit margin is 40%.

Important Considerations

This calculator uses revenue and cost of goods sold. It does not automatically include operating expenses, interest, income taxes or other indirect costs.

Make sure your revenue and costs relate to the same accounting period and use consistent accounting figures.
