Break-Even Calculator

Find out how many units you need to sell to cover your fixed costs and reach the break-even point.

How the Break-Even Point Is Calculated

The break-even point is the sales quantity at which total revenue equals total costs. At this point, there is no operating profit or loss under the assumptions used.

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price per Unit − Variable Cost per Unit)

Example Calculation

Imagine your business has these figures:

  • Selling price per unit: $50
  • Variable cost per unit: $20
  • Fixed costs: $3,000

Each unit contributes $30 toward fixed costs. Dividing $3,000 by $30 gives a break-even point of 100 units.

What to Check Before Using the Result

Fixed costs and variable costs should cover the same period. For example, if your fixed costs are monthly, the result represents the number of units to sell during that month.

This calculator assumes one product or service, a constant selling price and a constant variable cost per unit. It does not automatically include taxes, discounts, changing prices or multiple products.

Note: This calculator provides estimates for general business planning and educational purposes only. Results depend on the information entered and may not include every cost, tax or business circumstance. Verify important decisions with appropriate official sources or qualified professionals.
