Invoice Payment Terms: What Small Businesses Should Include

Clear invoice terms help customers understand when and how to pay. They also reduce avoidable questions and payment delays.

The basic formula

A clear invoice identifies the seller, customer, invoice number, issue date, due date, services or products, amounts, applicable tax and payment instructions.

A simple example

State the due date plainly, explain accepted payment methods and include a contact for billing questions. Keep a copy of the invoice and record when payment arrives.

Practical tips

Legal requirements differ by jurisdiction. Check local invoicing and tax rules, especially for VAT or sales tax registration and required invoice fields.
