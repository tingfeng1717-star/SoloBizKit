Break-Even Analysis for Small Businesses

Break-even analysis estimates the sales level at which revenue covers fixed and variable costs, before profit.

The basic formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Selling price per unit − Variable cost per unit)

A simple example

With fixed costs of $3,000, a $50 selling price and $20 variable cost, contribution is $30 per unit. Break-even is 100 units.

Practical tips

Use consistent time periods for costs and sales. This simplified formula assumes a stable selling price and variable cost; multiple products require a weighted contribution margin.
