Cash Flow Basics for Freelancers and Small Businesses

Cash flow is the movement of money into and out of a business. A profitable business can still face cash shortages if customers pay late or expenses arrive first.

The basic formula

Net cash flow = Cash received − Cash paid

A simple example

Prepare a short rolling forecast of expected customer receipts, payroll, supplier bills, tax payments and other commitments. Update it when invoices are paid or dates change.

Practical tips

Invoice promptly, agree payment terms in writing, follow up on overdue invoices and keep a buffer for predictable costs. Forecast cash timing, not just accounting profit.
