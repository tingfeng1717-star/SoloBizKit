Business Expenses Freelancers Should Track

Tracking expenses helps owners understand profitability, prepare records and make better spending decisions.

The basic formula

Useful categories include software, equipment, workspace, travel, marketing, professional services, payment fees and supplies.

A simple example

Save receipts and note the business purpose, date, supplier and amount. Reconcile records regularly instead of waiting until year-end.

Practical tips

Deductibility and record-retention rules vary by country. Ask a qualified tax professional how to classify expenses in your jurisdiction.

Put it into practice

Use the free SoloBizKit calculators to test your assumptions and compare different scenarios. Keep your inputs and assumptions up to date as your business changes.
