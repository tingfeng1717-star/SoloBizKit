Sales Tax and VAT: A Practical Introduction

Sales tax and value-added tax are consumption taxes, but their rules and collection mechanisms differ across countries and regions.

The basic formula

Tax amount = Taxable amount × Applicable rate

A simple example

A simple estimate applies a percentage to a taxable amount. Real obligations depend on product type, location, registration status, exemptions and cross-border rules.

Practical tips

Treat calculator outputs as estimates, not tax advice. Confirm current rules with the relevant tax authority or an accountant before charging or filing tax.
