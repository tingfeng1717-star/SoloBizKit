Markup vs. Margin: What's the Difference?

Markup and margin both describe the relationship between cost and selling price, but they use different denominators. Markup is based on cost; margin is based on selling price.

The basic formula

Markup = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100; Margin = (Selling price − Cost) ÷ Selling price × 100

A simple example

If an item costs $60 and sells for $100, the markup is 66.7% while the gross margin is 40%. The dollar profit is the same, but the percentages answer different questions.

Practical tips

Use markup when building a price from cost. Use margin when reviewing how much revenue remains as gross profit. Avoid treating the two percentages as interchangeable.
