About SoloBizKit

SoloBizKit makes practical business calculations and learning resources easier to access. Our goal is to help freelancers, solopreneurs and small businesses make everyday decisions with clearer numbers.

Our calculators are designed to be simple, transparent and useful on desktop and mobile. They provide estimates for learning and planning; they do not replace professional financial, tax or legal advice.

We aim to publish straightforward tools and guides for an international English-speaking audience. Always check local rules and seek qualified advice for important decisions.
