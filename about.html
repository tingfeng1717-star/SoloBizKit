OUR MISSION

About SoloBizKit

Welcome to SoloBizKit, a practical online resource designed to help freelancers, solopreneurs, creators and small business owners make more informed everyday business decisions.

What We Do

Running a business often involves making decisions about pricing, costs, profit, invoices, taxes and cash flow. These calculations can take time and become confusing.

SoloBizKit provides simple online calculators and educational guides to make common business calculations easier to understand and use.

Our Tools

Our website offers practical tools covering topics such as:

  • Profit margins and business markups
  • Break-even points and operating costs
  • Freelance hourly rates and project pricing
  • Invoice calculations and sales tax estimates
  • Loan payment estimates

Our Approach

We believe useful business tools should be straightforward, accessible and easy to understand. Our calculators are designed to work on desktop and mobile devices.

We aim to explain calculations clearly and provide practical educational content that helps visitors understand the numbers behind their decisions.

Accuracy and Limitations

We make reasonable efforts to keep our tools and guides useful and accurate. However, calculator results are estimates based on the information entered by visitors. They should not be treated as professional financial, tax, accounting or legal advice.

Tax rules, regulations and business requirements vary by country and may change. Please verify important information with appropriate official sources or qualified professionals.

Our Commitment

We aim to keep improving SoloBizKit by maintaining existing tools, publishing helpful guides and making the website easier to use.
